This Privacy Policy describes how HARMAN Field Force ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. The app is provided for authorized sales representatives of HARMAN. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide app functionality:

  • Account information. Mobile number and user credentials used for login.
  • Sales data. Product details, distributor information, invoice numbers, serial numbers, quantity, and sale dates entered by you.
  • Customer information. Customer name and mobile number (optional fields) entered during sale entry.
  • Invoice images. Photos captured via the camera for invoice upload.
  • Attendance and location data. GPS location (precise and, where available, approximate) captured when you mark attendance, check out, or upload an invoice photo. After you mark Present at an assigned store, a store geofence may remain active for that visit. If you grant background location, location may be used while the app is minimized, closed, or the phone screen is locked, so the app can verify your physical presence at the assigned store, record arrival and departure, and support field visit monitoring until checkout or end of the working day.
  • Device information. Device model, OS version, app version, network status, and basic phone state, used for support, compatibility, and secure sign-in.
  • Notifications. A push token so we can send account and sales notifications to your device.

We do not sell this information. We do not use it for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and manage your account.
  • To record and process sales entries submitted by you.
  • To calculate and display incentive points and passbook data.
  • To upload and store invoice images securely on our servers.
  • To record attendance, checkout, and field location related to your work.
  • To verify your physical presence at assigned stores during scheduled visits, including when the app is in the background.
  • To provide target versus achievement reports.
  • To send notifications related to your account or sales activity.
  • To show maps and Help Line contact actions inside the app.
  • To improve app performance and fix issues.

10. Location Permissions Declaration

HARMAN Field Force meets the Google Play Location permissions policy. Location access is limited to authorized field-force operations for store attendance, visit monitoring, and related business features described below.

Policy compliance: Yes, this app meets the Location permissions policy.

11. App Purpose

HARMAN Field Force is an enterprise app for authorized HARMAN sales representatives. It manages store attendance, checkout, sales entry, invoice capture, incentives, and field visit monitoring. Background location is used only after mark-in to verify the representative remains at the assigned store (geofence), including when the app is minimized or the screen is locked, until checkout or end of day.

12. Location-Based Feature

HARMAN Field Force uses background location to verify whether authorized sales representatives are physically present at their assigned stores during scheduled visits. Location access continues when the app is minimized or the phone screen is locked, allowing the system to record store arrival and departure and verify attendance. This is essential for field visit monitoring and attendance verification. Location is used only for authorized business operations.

After a Present mark-in, the app creates a store geofence (approximately 200 meters) around the assigned store coordinates. Enter and exit events verify presence even when the app is in the background or the screen is locked. The geofence ends at checkout or at the end of the working day.

13. Prominent Disclosure

Before the Android runtime permission prompt, the app shows the following in-app disclosure and requires affirmative consent:

Background Location Access HARMAN Field Force collects your location even when the app is not in use or your phone screen is locked. This allows HARMAN to verify your presence at assigned stores, monitor store visits, and record attendance. Your location is used for authorized field-force operations only. By continuing, you acknowledge and consent to this location access.

After the user taps Agree, the system location permission dialog is shown. Location is not requested without this consent step.

15. Location Data Types

Precise location

Used to mark attendance and checkout, capture GPS with invoice photo upload, and verify the sales representative’s physical presence at the assigned store during a scheduled visit (including geofence enter/exit).

Approximate location

Used as a fallback network-based location when precise GPS is unavailable, for the same attendance, checkout, and store-presence verification purposes.

Background location

Used after mark-in to verify presence at the assigned store while the app is minimized, closed, or the screen is locked. A store geofence records arrival and departure for field visit monitoring until checkout or end of the working day. Not used for advertising.

16. Location Monitoring Scope

When location is monitored

Only near or during an assigned store visit Location is monitored through a store geofence around the assigned store after Present mark-in. The app does not continuously track the employee everywhere.

When monitoring is active

Only during working hours / assigned store visits Monitoring starts after Present mark-in, expires at the end of the working day, and is removed on checkout. Location is not monitored 24 hours a day outside assigned store visits.
