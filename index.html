Privacy Policy

HARMAN Field Force  ·  Android app  ·  Effective date: 7 November 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how HARMAN Field Force ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application. The app is provided for authorized sales representatives of Usha International Limited. By using the app, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide app functionality:

  • Account information. Mobile number and user credentials used for login.
  • Sales data. Product details, distributor information, invoice numbers, serial numbers, quantity, and sale dates entered by you.
  • Customer information. Customer name and mobile number (optional fields) entered during sale entry.
  • Invoice images. Photos captured via the camera for invoice upload.
  • Attendance and location data. GPS location (precise and, where available, approximate) captured when you mark attendance, check out, upload an invoice photo, or when a work geofence is active. If you grant background location, location may also be collected while the app is not open, so attendance and geofence features can continue.
  • Device information. Device model, OS version, app version, network status, and basic phone state, used for support, compatibility, and secure sign-in.
  • Notifications. A push token so we can send account and sales notifications to your device.

We do not sell this information. We do not use it for advertising.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate and manage your account.
  • To record and process sales entries submitted by you.
  • To calculate and display incentive points and passbook data.
  • To upload and store invoice images securely on our servers.
  • To record attendance, checkout, and field location related to your work.
  • To provide target versus achievement reports.
  • To send notifications related to your account or sales activity.
  • To show maps and Help Line contact actions inside the app.
  • To improve app performance and fix issues.

3. Permissions

The app requests only the Android permissions needed for the features below. You can deny a permission in system settings. Some features, such as invoice capture, attendance, or notifications, will not work without the related permission.

Permission Why the app uses it
Camera Capture invoice images and scan barcodes or QR codes for serial number entry.
Photos, media, and storage
(Read / write external storage, photos and media)		 Save and access invoice images on your device so they can be uploaded.
Precise location
(ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)		 Record GPS location for attendance, checkout, and invoice photo upload.

(ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION)		 Use network-based location when precise GPS is unavailable.

Internet and network state Send sales, attendance, and invoice data to our servers and check whether the device is online.

(WRITE_SETTINGS)		 Used only if a device setting must be adjusted for a feature to work correctly. It is not used to change unrelated settings.

These permissions are used solely for the features described above and are not used for any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Your data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • With Usha International Limited’s internal systems for sales processing, attendance, and incentive calculation.
  • With authorized distributors relevant to your sales entries.
  • With service providers that process data on our behalf to run the app: Google Play services, Google Maps, and Google push notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging / Google Cloud Messaging). These providers receive only what is needed for maps, location services, and notification delivery.
  • When required by law or legal process.

5. Data Storage and Security

Sales, account, attendance, and invoice data are stored on secure servers managed by Usha International Limited. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

Data sent to our servers is transmitted over HTTPS wherever the service supports it. Keep your login credentials private and do not share your device with unauthorized people while you are signed in.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account is active or as required for business and legal purposes, including sales records, attendance, and incentive calculation.

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data by contacting us using the details in section 9. We will delete or anonymize personal data that we are not required to keep for legal, audit, or incentive-settlement reasons. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from that device. It does not by itself delete records already submitted to our servers.

7. Children’s Privacy

This app is intended for use by authorized sales representatives of Usha International Limited. It is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
